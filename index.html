About Jhustin Hall

Jhustin began life in North Carolina as a country boy. He is part of a large, musically talented family with God fearing parents. Saved and baptized with the Holy Ghost while attending a revival in his hometown, he is a self-taught bassist, guitarist and drummer with amazing musical talent. His music, influenced by many genres, is an eclectic blend of gospel, soul, reggae and country. He answered God’s calling and is fulfilling his purpose through music, preaching, teaching and servanthood.

His innate creativity brings both national and international fame as an independent Christian artist. Trials and tribulations are the inspiration behind the heartfelt songs. He is evolving into a well-rounded musician who not only sings but writes, plays and produces music for God’s glory. Starting off as an independent artist, he worked his way to the top, including producing his own record label. In addition, Jhustin is an entrepreneur and a published author.
