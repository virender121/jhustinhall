THE HOLLY GHOST FELL ON ME

Leaning into his faith, his first solo album was deeply personal. "I came to a place of contentment with God, no longer concerned with the excitement of the big lights and the thrill of rising up the billboards. The challanges of being entrenched in the business side of music industry became a distraction."

Immediately after releasing his first album in 2016, The Holy Ghost Fell On Me, Jhustin embarked on a international tour in Puerto Rico. The one-month tour was a huge success, and landed him two top hits, Just a Little Bit of Love and turn it over to Jesus. He quickly became well known on the island and sold thousands of albums, opening great opportunities to tour with Latin Singers such as Alex Campus, Jesus Adrian Romero and many more. The Holy Ghost Fell On Me spent seven weeks on the charts there, Jump starting his solo career.
